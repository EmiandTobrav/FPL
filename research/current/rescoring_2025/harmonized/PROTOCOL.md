# Harmonized defensive counts protocol

Declared 2026-10-07, before full-season contemporary validation is available.

Scope: 2019/20–2025/26, 380 official fixtures each. One FotMob definition of clearances, shot blocks, interceptions, total tackles and recoveries; no raw FBref clearance substitution. Dates, both sides, final score and stable Opta/FPL code must match. Statistics that differ after identity is established are retained as explicit revision flags, not silently corrected FPL minutes/goals. Unknown counts stay missing; a nonnegative known subtotal already above the threshold can establish an award. Goalkeepers and no-minute rows get no defcon award.

The five reconstructed components are post-match targets, never pre-deadline features. Future outcome revisions must not leak into feature construction or rolling training cutoffs. Keep all archived models and benchmark results untouched.

2025/26 official FPL awards and total points are authoritative. Reconstructed provider counts are compared with these on the full active-outfield season; report missingness, count agreement, false positives and false negatives, including position and gameweek breakdowns. Targeted earlier discrepancy studies are not reused as unbiased accuracy estimates.

For a frozen research scoring dataset, require 380 uniquely identified source matches per season, no unresolved historical award labels, invariant and identity checks, and at least 99.0% award agreement against official 2025/26 on comparable active-outfield rows. Require full contemporary source coverage or explicitly resolve any exceptions before passing the gate. These are research acceptance criteria, not proof of exact historical FPL counterfactual points. If the criteria fail, retain an unlocked candidate and diagnose the failure.

Older season scoring is original FPL points plus reconstructed defcon. 2025/26 total points remain unchanged: never add defcon twice. Historical assists and bonus have not been converted to every modern rule, so the version must be named and described as historical points plus harmonized defcon. A successful lock freezes data/definitions; it does not promote a model or establish a new season-score benchmark.

Persist per-season normalized counts, labels, source URLs/hashes, input hashes, all exceptions, validation and deterministic builders. Keep old data versions unchanged and report changed awards and population point deltas against the preceding five repaired seasons. No paid data or subscriptions; NOK 500 remains the total ceiling for any later purchase.
